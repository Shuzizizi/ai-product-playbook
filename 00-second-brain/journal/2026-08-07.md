## Sprint 1 Day 1 Reflection — Understanding Artificial Intelligence

### What I learned

I learned that AI is not simply about replacing human work or automating tasks. The key difference between traditional software and AI is how the solution is created.

Traditional software relies on predefined rules:
- Humans define the logic
- Systems execute the rules

AI learns patterns from data:
- Models identify patterns
- Systems generate predictions or outputs based on learned behavior


### How my thinking changed

Before learning about AI, I mainly viewed AI as a technology that can automate tasks or provide faster answers.

After this learning, I understand that AI creates value differently:
- It can handle complex patterns that are difficult to define with rules
- It can enhance human decision-making
- It can support people in areas requiring analysis, creativity, and knowledge processing


### Product Owner Perspective

As a Product Owner, the key question is not:

"Where can we add AI?"

The better question is:

"What problem are we trying to solve, and is AI the right approach?"

AI opportunities should start from:
- User problems
- Business value
- Available data
- Expected outcomes

Not from the technology itself.


### Connection to my work

In enterprise products, many problems can be solved through traditional automation and rules.

However, AI may create additional value when:
- The problem involves complex patterns
- Rules become difficult to maintain
- Users need assistance with analysis or decision-making


### Open Questions

- How do we identify whether a problem is suitable for AI?
- When should we choose AI instead of traditional automation?
- How can enterprise data be safely used with AI?
