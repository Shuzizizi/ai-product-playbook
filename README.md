# ai-product-playbook
Exploration journey learning AI, Agentic AI and AI Product Management.

## Why this exists

AI is transforming how products are designed, built, and delivered.

As a Business Analyst transitioning into a Product Owner role, I want to build a deeper understanding of AI — not only as a technology, but as a product capability.

This repository documents my journey to explore AI concepts, experiment with AI solutions, and develop the ability to identify where AI can create meaningful business value.

My goal is to bridge the gap between:

* Business problems
* Product thinking
* AI capabilities
* Enterprise implementation

---

# Product Vision

## Building AI Product Thinking Capability

Enable Product Owners and Business Analysts to identify, evaluate, and design AI-powered solutions through structured product thinking.

The vision is to develop the knowledge and practical experience required to answer:

* Where can AI create real value?
* When should we use AI versus traditional automation?
* Which AI approach is suitable (LLM, RAG, Workflow, Agent)?
* How can AI solutions be delivered responsibly in an enterprise environment?

---

# Learning Goals

## 1. Build AI Literacy

Develop a strong foundation of AI concepts:

* Large Language Models (LLMs)
* Prompt Engineering
* Embeddings
* Vector Databases
* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
* AI Agents
* Tool Calling
* AI Governance

The goal is not to become an AI engineer, but to understand AI capabilities and limitations from a product perspective.

---

## 2. Develop AI Product Skills

Learn how to translate business problems into AI opportunities:

* Identify pain points
* Evaluate AI suitability
* Define user value
* Estimate business impact
* Understand risks and limitations
* Create AI product roadmaps

---

## 3. Build Practical Experience

Create small AI experiments and prototypes to understand:

* How AI applications are built
* How different AI patterns work
* When to use different approaches

Examples:

* Knowledge Assistant
* Requirement Analysis Assistant
* Release Note Generator
* Product Owner AI Coach

# AI Product Principles

Throughout this journey, I will focus on these principles:

## AI is not always the answer

The goal is not to add AI everywhere.

The goal is to solve meaningful problems.

---

## Start with problems, not technology

Every AI idea should begin with:

* User problem
* Business value
* Expected outcome

---

## Human + AI collaboration

AI should enhance human capability, not blindly replace human decision-making.

---

## Enterprise readiness matters

A successful AI product requires:

* Data availability
* Security
* Governance
* Adoption
* Change management
